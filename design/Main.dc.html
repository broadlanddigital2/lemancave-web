03 / ABOUT LEMAN CAVE

Built by
racing fans

LeMan Cave started with a simple idea: the thrill of race day shouldn’t need a paddock pass.

Today it’s a home for serious sim drivers, first-timers and everyone in between — a place to compete, catch up and celebrate, wrapped in the sights and sounds of motorsport.

Even if you’re a regular at circuit racing and track days, it’s a great way to learn a course before that daunting first lap out. We regularly get visitors using the sims before their dream European trip to Spa, Zandvoort and the Nürburgring, to learn the corners and make the experience more enjoyable.

LeMan Cave is a purpose-built motorsport den in Norwich. Low light, loud engines and walls lined with racing memorabilia — it’s where petrolheads, first-timers and office leagues come to settle scores.

Fully air-conditioned, with a Sonos sound system and ambient lighting throughout. Add pizza*, bring your own drinks, and make the place yours.
