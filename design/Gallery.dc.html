Racing simulator venue photos

The racing photos show the heart of the cave: pro racing simulators (one with full motion) with direct-drive wheels (the full-motion sim in 4K or VR, the static sim on an HD monitor), the 160ft digital Scalextric (up to six cars) and the classic 1990s motorbike sim. The simulators may have height restrictions, and motion may be disabled for safety for guests under 14.

Party venue photos from Norwich

For parties the whole cave is yours: around 50 guests across 1,600 sq ft, with the lounge, the games consoles on a 100-inch screen, pool, darts, pinball, air hockey and arcade games alongside the racing. Add pizza* and Bring Your Own drinks, and finish with a podium for the winners.
