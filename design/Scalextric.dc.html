01 / THE TRACK

Digital Scalextric: not the set under your tree

Our digital circuit winds 160ft through the cave, past a lit pit lane and live timing screens. Everyone races at once, every lap is timed, and the leaderboard never lies.

It’s the perfect warm-up before the simulators — or the main event for groups who just want to battle it out together.
